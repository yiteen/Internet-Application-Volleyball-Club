Our Club History

The APU Volleyball Club was established in 2010 by a small group of passionate students who wanted to promote volleyball on campus. Over the years, the club has grown significantly in both membership and achievements, becoming one of the most active sports clubs at APU.

From hosting internal tournaments to participating in inter-university competitions, the club has continually fostered athletic excellence, teamwork, and leadership among its members. Experienced players and alumni have played a crucial role in mentoring new members and maintaining a culture of discipline and sportsmanship.

Throughout the years, APU Volleyball Club has expanded its programs to include beginner training, skill workshops, social events, and collaborative projects with other student organizations. This has helped build a supportive community where members can improve both on and off the court, creating a legacy of excellence and camaraderie.


Milestones

2018 - Club Establishment

The APU Volleyball Club was officially formed with its first batch of enthusiastic students.


2019 - First Internal Tournament

The club organized its first internal volleyball tournament to build team spirit.


2021 - Certified Coaching & Structured Training:

To improve player development, the club introduced training sessions led by certified coaches, focusing on technique, fitness, and strategic play.


2022 - Inter-University Participation:

The club actively took part in inter-university competitions, gaining valuable match experience and achieving notable performances.


2025 - MASISWA Championship Success:

The APU Men's Volleyball Team secured the 2nd Runner-up position at the MASISWA Major Sports Championship 2025, showcasing their growth and competitive spirit on a national level.
