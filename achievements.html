Club Milestones

2018 - Club Establishment:

The APU Volleyball Club was officially formed with its first batch of enthusiastic students who shared a passion for volleyball and community building.


2019 - First Internal Tournament:

The club organized its first internal volleyball tournament, allowing both new and senior members to interact, compete, and build stronger team spirit.


2021 - Certified Coaching & Structured Training:

To improve player development, the club introduced training sessions led by certified coaches, focusing on technique, fitness, and strategic play.


2022 - Inter-University Participation:

The club actively took part in inter-university competitions, gaining valuable match experience and achieving notable performances.


2024 - Advanced Programs & Collaborations:


New advanced training programs were introduced, and the club collaborated with other university sports teams to organize joint events and skill-sharing workshops.
