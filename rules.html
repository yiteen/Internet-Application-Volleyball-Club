Club Rules & Guidelines

All APU Volleyball Club members are expected to follow these rules for a safe and respectful environment:

  1. Respect

    Treat all members, coaches, and opponents with respect both on and off the court.

  2. Punctuality

    Arrive on time for training sessions, matches, and events.

  3. Attendance

    Regular attendance is expected. Inform the coach in advance if unable to attend.

  4. Sportsmanship

    Demonstrate fair play, positivity, and encourage teammates during practices.

  5. Safety

    Follow safety instructions, use proper equipment, and avoid dangerous behavior.

  6. Commitment

    Participate actively in club activities and strive to improve skills.

  7. Uniform

    Wear the appropriate club attire for training and matches.

  8. Cleanliness

    Keep the training area and equipment clean and organized.

  9. Prohibited Behavior

    Bullying, harassment, or discrimination is strictly forbidden.

  10. Feedback

    Provide constructive feedback and communicate openly with coaches.
