Our Coaches

Coach Lee Yan Kang

With over 8 years of professional coaching experience, Coach Lee has guided numerous university volleyball teams to championship victories. He specializes in advanced offensive and defensive techniques, tactical game strategies, and player development. His approach emphasizes both physical conditioning and mental toughness, ensuring players excel under competitive pressure.

Achievements:

  • Led APU team to win Inter-University Volleyball Championship 2022.
  • Certified Level 3 Volleyball Coach.
  • Organized coaching workshops for regional high school teams.

Coach Lee is passionate about mentoring students to reach their full potential and fostering a supportive team culture.

Coach Susan Lim

Coach Susan has extensive experience in developing beginner and intermediate players. Her training focuses on building strong foundational skills, coordination, and teamwork. She believes in a holistic approach that balances skill acquisition with character development.

Achievements:

  • Head Coach for multiple university-level beginner programs.
  • Recipient of "Best Coach Award" at Regional Volleyball Meet 2021.
  • Organized community volleyball clinics for youth development.

Coach Susan encourages a positive learning environment where players can grow with confidence, enjoy the sport, and develop leadership skills on and off the court.
