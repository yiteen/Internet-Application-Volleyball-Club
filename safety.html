Safety Guidelines for All Members

At APU Volleyball Club, member safety is our priority. Please follow these guidelines for a safe experience.

Warm-Up & Stretching

Always perform proper warm-up and stretching exercises before training or matches.

Proper Attire & Equipment

Wear appropriate sports shoes, clothing, and protective gear during training and competitions.

Hydration

Drink enough water before, during, and after sessions to avoid dehydration.

Playing Area

Keep courts and surrounding areas clear of obstacles. Report any hazards immediately.

Safe Techniques

Learn and practice correct techniques to prevent injuries.

Emergency Protocols

Familiarize yourself with emergency exits, first aid kits, and contact persons in case of accidents.

Health Conditions

Inform coaches about any pre-existing medical conditions or injuries.

Respect Boundaries

Avoid rough or unsafe play that may endanger yourself or others.

Supervision

Always train under the guidance of coaches or senior players.

Report Incidents

Report any injuries, accidents, or unsafe practices immediately to club officials.
