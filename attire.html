Required Attire & Equipment

To ensure safe and effective training, all members of the APU Volleyball Club are required to wear appropriate attire and have access to basic equipment. Wearing the correct gear not only enhances performance but also reduces the risk of injury during practice and matches.


Training Attire

  • Sports T-shirt Lightweight and breathable clothing to ensure comfort during intense training
  • Sports Shorts Allows free movement and agility
  • Indoor Court Shoes Provides proper grip on the court and prevents slipping
  • Knee Pads Protects the knees during dives and falls

Recommended Equipment

  • Water Bottle Stay hydrated throughout training
  • Volleyball For individual skill development outside team sessions
  • Armsleeves Help absorb sweat and improve grip
  • Ankle Guards Provides extra support for players prone to ankle injuries
